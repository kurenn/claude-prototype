# Brief: specialty coffee subscription (brand register)

Feed this verbatim to `/prototype` (choose **Quick** mode), then score the output folder.
No inspiration is named on purpose: this brief tests a consumer brand site in a domain the
corpus doesn't cover (retail), where the skill has the least to go on.

> Prototype **Halfmoon Roasters**, a small specialty-coffee roaster that sells mostly by
> subscription. Tone: warm, editorial, a little playful. Audience: a pitch to the founders.
> Scope: 4 screens — Home (the brand story and this month's coffees), Shop (filterable by
> roast, origin and brew method), Subscription builder (pick coffees, grind, size and
> cadence with a live price), Account (next delivery, skip/pause/swap, order history).
> Content: realistic placeholder — real-sounding farms, origins, tasting notes and prices.

Expected from a good run: 4 screens; a brand voice that isn't generic e-commerce; the
builder's price updating live and validating impossible combinations; skip/pause with
confirmation and undo; an empty order history state for a new customer; no stock-photo
clichés (use the sanctioned `.media` placeholder).
