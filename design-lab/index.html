Audit 02 — in progress ▸ tint every neutral, commit to a strategy

Color

The scaffold ships flat, untinted grays today (--muted: 120 120 120) — the "dead gray" tell both doctrines ban. The fix: derive neutrals in OKLCH and tint them toward the palette's hue, commit to one accent, and pick a strategy. Drag the hue below to feel the difference.

  • OKLCH neutrals tinted 0.005–0.015 toward the hue
  • Never #000 / #fff / chroma-0 gray
  • One accent ≤ 10% (restrained)
  • Dark = lighter surfaces, not shadow; desaturate the accent
  • Category-reflex check — not guessable from the domain
Flat · chroma 0 — dead
Tinted · chroma .013 → hue
Tint hue 150°

Same lightness on both rows — the only difference is a whisper of chroma. The tinted ramp reads as chosen; the flat one reads as default. Every surface, border, and muted text derives from it.

Color strategy — commit before you pick

Pick a position on the commitment axis first; it decides how much color the surface carries. Most product UI is restrained; brand/landing pages earn more.

Restrainedtinted neutrals + one accent ≤10%
Committedone color carries 30–60%
Full palette3–4 named roles, each deliberate
Drenchedthe surface IS the color

The curated palettes — a menu by tone

Same idea as the font pairings: one committed, tinted, category-reflex-avoiding palette per tone, light + dark. The skill recommends by tone.

technical · restrained

Pine on warm graphite — green on a warm off-white, no blue-on-black.

corporate · restrained

Aubergine on warm stone — confident and human, zero boardroom navy.

editorial · committed

Signal red on cool newsprint — deliberately the opposite of terracotta-on-cream.

playful · committed

Marigold on periwinkle paper — one warm pop against a cool ground, not candy chaos.

bold-experimental · drenched

Acid lime on petrol teal — loud and saturated, but a teal base, not neon-on-black.

minimal · restrained

Monochrome ink-blue — even the grays are tinted; the accent is a deeper shade of the same hue.

Each row: light theme (top) and dark (bottom), running surface → ink then the accent. All six clear 4.5:1 for body ink in both themes. Two honest flags from the research: ink-3 (captions/timestamps) is AA-Large only in light mode — never set body copy in it; and the playful marigold / bold lime accents are fill roles (button background with dark text), so darken them for text links.
